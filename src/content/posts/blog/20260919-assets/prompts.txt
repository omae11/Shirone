## gray-box.png

Use case: scientific-educational. Create a polished editorial illustration for a Chinese study blog about ordinary human visual perception. Landscape 1536x1024. A single unmarked matte gray rectangular utility storage box sitting on a concrete floor immediately in front of an aged gray concrete wall, three quarter view. Box paint and wall have similar middle gray hue, but box has straight crisp edges and smooth surface, wall has subtle mottling and fine cracks. Soft directional light from upper right casts a coherent visible shadow to lower left. Restrained realistic architectural illustration, subtle paper texture, warm neutral gray palette, clear informative composition, box centered and fully visible, generous surrounding wall. No people, no military objects, no camouflage net, no text, no labels, no arrows, no watermark. This illustrates why matching color does not erase shape, texture and shadow cues, not an experiment or concealment design.
